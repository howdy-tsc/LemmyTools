Site Style and Behavior

Open links in new tab “External” opens only links that leave the current site. “All” also forces internal Lemmy navigation into a new tab.

Community Groups

Organize your subscribed communities into named, collapsible groups in the LemmyTools bar. Use the search below to find a community, then click a group chip to add or remove it. Manage the list of groups in the bar at the bottom of this section.

Discovered Communities

Communities you've encountered while browsing other Lemmy instances. They appear in the LemmyTools bar with a one-click "Browse/Sub on home" link.
